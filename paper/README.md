### Final claim boundary

The paper derives the identity, ultraheavy mass, exact `q -> -q` stability, cold/collisionless dynamics, and existing scalar-portal pair-production operator of the retained heavy-`q` dark state. The absolute asymptotic abundance remains conditional on one universal post-handoff yield `Y_q`, because the frozen `2+1 -> 3+1` handoff does not yet uniquely derive the channel-resolved loading, the post-handoff Phi-visible thermal connector, or the threshold-complete Pati-Salam thermal spectrum.

No first-principles absolute-abundance claim is made in v1.0.0.
